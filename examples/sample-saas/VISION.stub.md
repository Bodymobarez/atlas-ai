# Vision — Northstar Attendance (STUB EXAMPLE)

**Status:** Example only — not a real approved vision

## Vision Statement

Help small teams record who showed up for each shift without spreadsheets.

## Target Customer

Operations managers in companies with 20–200 deskless workers.

## MVP Promise

A manager creates today’s shifts; employees check in with a link; manager exports a CSV of attendance.

## Out of Scope for MVP

Payroll, geofencing, biometric hardware, mobile native apps (phase 2).
